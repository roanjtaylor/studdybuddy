# studybuddy

Study the core ideas of a field, not the infinite examples of them. Most of the results in any field come from a small number of ideas; this is a place to find those ideas, see them all at once, and own them one by one.

## What it is

A **map** of a field: its groups of ideas, each compressed to one sentence, with what it builds on and what it leads to. The map shows the whole breadth of the field, and on top of it, your understanding of it: what you can explain, what is shaky, what you have not touched.

A **study buddy** that lives inside the map. Everything you say to it is sent with the map, the idea you are looking at and what you have written so far, so every answer is about *your* understanding of *this* field. It can answer the most niche question, quiz you, set you questions, explain an idea from first principles, and record what you have learned.

The same buddy **reshapes the map** when you ask: add what is missing, go deeper on one group, merge or regroup, remove the noise, show the field as a timeline or an outline instead of a map. You are never confined to a chat; the map is the shared object, and both of you edit it. Anything it does, you can also do by hand.

## The loop

1. **Create a field.** Name it, say what you already know and why you want it. The buddy researches it and lays out the core ideas breadth first, in prerequisite order.
2. **Look at the whole thing.** Switch between the map, an outline and a timeline. Focus on one group or one idea's neighbourhood. Ask what is missing and where you are weak.
3. **Go deep where it matters.** Open an idea. Ask about it. Expand it into its sub-ideas. Write your explanation cold and have it checked: right, missing, wrong. Rate yourself. Watch the map change colour.
4. **Hone it over time.** The map morphs around your understanding: ideas get notes, groups get reshaped, levels rise. Come back and ask what to do next.

## Rules

- Core ideas, not examples. One idea, one sentence.
- Theory from the originator, products by using them.
- Nothing is understood until written out and checked. The buddy gives evidence; you set your level.
